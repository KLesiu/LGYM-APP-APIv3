# LgymApi.Identity.csproj

- Purpose: stable code boundary for identity and account capabilities.
- Contains: the `AccountReference`, `AccountSessionReference`, and `RoleReference` contract-only typed-ID markers; focused account workflows and public account facts; internal repositories, EF configurations, and authentication providers.
- Public account surface: marker-only account lookup, account-access facts, session validation, authenticated-account-context contracts, the narrow `IAuthenticatedAccountCompatibilityPort`, and the stage-only `IAccountSessionDisassociationPort` and `IAccountPushInstallationCleanupPort`. These contracts expose scalar facts and marker IDs only; `User`, `Role`, repositories, credential material, and provider details remain internal.
- Rules: keep direct references limited to Domain, Platform, and Resources. Its internal persistence seam is friend-visible only to Infrastructure, UnitTests, and IntegrationTests. `IdentitySeedIds` publishes the stable role and claim identifiers without exposing entities or persistence internals.
- Boundary: do not reference Notifications, Infrastructure, Worker, or create a persistence root. Session termination revokes the Identity session and invokes its marker-only disassociation port before its single UoW commit; account anonymization similarly stages PushInstallation cleanup before the same commit. Notifications supplies both stage-only adapters. `IIdentityPersistenceContext` exposes only the nine Identity-owned sets and the read-only provider name; it has no save, transaction, database, or generic-entry access.
- EF coordination: `IdentityModelConfigurationRegistrar` is the first fixed model-registration phase and explicitly applies the nine Identity configurations without assembly scanning.
- Identity owns its concrete repositories, PBKDF2/JWT/Google/session providers, default-options resolver, role/claim seed metadata, and Identity registrations. Implementations use only `IIdentityPersistenceContext`; Infrastructure retains the one `AppDbContext`, scoped context alias, UoW, conventions, migrations, and fixed global registrar.
- `ILegacyPasswordService` remains directly scoped to `LegacyPasswordService`; the retired `LegacyPasswordServiceFactory` must not be restored.
- Identity business services use direct constructor injection. High constructor arity is acceptable for a focused service, but dependency aggregates and service location are forbidden.
- Session validation for authenticated account resolution is pair-aware: a session must be active, unexpired, undeleted, unrevoked, and owned by the claimed account. This keeps mismatched JWT `userId`/`sid` pairs from becoming active non-HTTP contexts.
- Every tutorial-progress operation begins the Platform actor row-security scope with the Identity user ID rebound to `ActorReference` before persistence access. Tutorial reads and no-op checks are no-tracking and dispose the scope without commit or rollback; actual tutorial mutations save once and then commit the actor scope once.
- The tutorial RLS pilot protects only the tutorial parent and child tables and remains staging-only. Its operational gate and rollback procedure are in [`tutorial-rls-pilot.md`](../docs/security/tutorial-rls-pilot.md); Application authorization remains required.
