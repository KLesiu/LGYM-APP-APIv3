# LgymApi.TrainingPlanning.csproj

- Purpose: stable code boundary for training-plan definition and planning workflows.
- Contains: the `PlanReference`, `PlanDayReference`, and consumer-owned `PlanExerciseReference` contract-only typed-ID markers; focused Plan, ManagedPlan, and PlanDay workflows; internal planning repositories and EF configurations.
- Rules: keep direct references limited to Domain, Identity, Platform, and Resources. Its internal persistence seam is friend-visible only to Infrastructure, UnitTests, and IntegrationTests.
- Boundary: do not reference Infrastructure or Worker, create a DbContext, or take ownership of completed workout execution. `ITrainingPlanningPersistenceContext` exposes only Plans, PlanDays, and PlanDayExercises.
- EF coordination: `TrainingPlanningModelConfigurationRegistrar` owns and explicitly applies the three Plan, PlanDay, and PlanDayExercise configurations as the second fixed model-registration phase; Infrastructure invokes that registrar without assembly scanning.
- Focused Plan, ManagedPlan, and PlanDay workflows compile here. Their legacy `LgymApi.Application.TrainingPlanning.*` namespaces remain for source and payload compatibility, while `TrainingPlanningModule.AddTrainingPlanningModule` is the sole focused-workflow registration facade.
- PlanDay exposes marker-ID commands, queries, and immutable read/write models through `IPlanDayService`, plus immutable five-fact reference reads through `IPlanDayReferenceReadService`: `PlanDayId`, `PlanId`, `Name`, `Exists`, and `IsDeleted`. Reference batches replay caller order and duplicates; missing IDs return `Exists=false` facts while persisted soft-deleted rows return `Exists=true, IsDeleted=true`. Implementations and Planning persistence ports are internal. The command service retains authorization, soft-delete, transaction, and one-commit behavior.
- Training Planning consumes Workout-owned exercise catalog, exercise-clone, and training-activity facts only through `IPlanExerciseCatalogPort`, `IPlanExerciseClonePort`, and `IPlanTrainingActivityPort`. The ports use Planning/account markers and immutable models; Workout composition supplies their adapters while Planning services retain missing-item handling and transaction ownership.
- The module owns the scoped stage-only `IPlanRepository`, `IPlanDayRepository`, `IPlanDayExerciseRepository`, and `IActivePlanPointerStore` implementations. They use only `ITrainingPlanningPersistenceContext`; active-plan selection is derived from non-deleted active Plans rather than the Identity-owned `User.PlanId` field. The Task-15 `IPlanDayPersistence` adapter remains Infrastructure composition over these internal ports.
- Architecture guards scan every non-build module source and the retained Plan and ManagedPlan Application compatibility adapters. The boundary-debt registry and maximum are fixed at zero; marker-only public contracts may not expose `User`, `Id<User>`, role repositories, or Workout entities/repositories, while internal entity-typed persisted keys and EF relationship mappings remain valid.
